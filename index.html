Gernreich

Projects by Michael Carnright, each with its own page.

LaserMadeMusic

Musical instruments cut from 3mm Baltic birch plywood on a laser, and the files to build them. Trumpets, flutes, ocarinas, panpipes, bagpipe chanters, a lyre-harp, a kalimba and more, with the generators and tools behind them. The cutting and the playing are on the LaserMadeMusic channel. Open it

Hexagonal dice box

A finger-jointed hexagonal box in 3mm ply with seven hexagonal pockets from glued insert layers, for a polyhedral dice set, and a lid held by 2 × 1mm magnets. Open it

Win(d)Bag

A bagpipe tune player begun in BASIC on an IBM PCjr and written in Visual Basic 3 between 1995 and 2000, with its source and a web edition that plays the tunes in the browser. Open it

Gernreich writing

A script in which every letter is two steps on a square grid and every word is one unbroken line, with a live writer and reader, and Article 1 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights drawn as a single line. Open it

Coprime intervals

Coprime pairs as a grid of rays, as Turk's head knots and as the musical intervals inside one octave. Open it

Sixteen boolean functions

Every two-input logic function, with its expressions enumerated, the weights a small neural network uses for each, and every function applied to every pair. Open it

Living hinge

Parametric lattice-hinge patterns for a laser cutter, so a flat sheet will bend. Open it

Best bang for the buck

Which folders to clean up first on a full drive, and where to stop: the running-total curve and its point nearest the origin. Open it
